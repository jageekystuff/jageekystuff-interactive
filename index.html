🧪 Prototype v2.11

The camera tracking works with a temporary HIRO test marker . The stable model selection is Magmar and Arcanine for Blaine, plus Raichu, Electrode and Electabuzz for Lt. Surge. V2.11 adds two optional experimental model variants: Rapidash V2 (flame materials still need checking) and Electabuzz V2 (new source model with a skeletal rig). Original models and calibrations are preserved. Use the on-screen calibration controls to compare. We still use HIRO for testing until the physical cosplay cards have their own AR target.
