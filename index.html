🧪 Prototype v2.12-hfx-1

We're testing custom QR-markers for Blaine and Lt. Surge. Scan the QR on a trainer card to open its experience, then point your camera at the same printed QR-marker to summon a Pokémon.

Four Pokémon are currently available: Magmar and Arcanine for Blaine; Raichu and Electrode for Lt. Surge. Custom QR-marker tracking still needs to be validated on real phones. The HIRO test marker remains available as a fallback inside each AR experience.
