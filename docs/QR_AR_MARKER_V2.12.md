# V2.12 — QR + AR marker test (not final printing)

## Card dimensions (confirmed)

All coordinates are measured from the **top-left corner** of each 80 × 120 cm card.

| Element | X | Y | Width × height |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Entire card | 0 cm | 0 cm | 80 × 120 cm |
| Cosplay window | 9 cm | 18 cm | 62 × 50 cm |
| Right Web/AR module | 43 cm | 74 cm | 27 × 27 cm |
| **QR itself, including its WHITE quiet zone** | **50 cm** | **76 cm** | **15 × 15 cm** |
| Black AR frame around the QR | 48 cm | 74 cm | 19 × 19 cm |
| Caption zone (suggested, NOT final typography placement) | 43 cm | 94 cm | 27 × 5.2 cm |

Black frame is an **additional physical border OUTSIDE the QR** and does not change the user-specified size or X/Y of the QR. It is essential to this marker-based AR.js prototype. The QR remains exactly 15 × 15 cm including quiet zone.

## Workflow

1. QR opens `/pokemon/blaine/` or `/pokemon/surge/` on the same phone.
2. User authorizes the phone camera and points it again at the same printed QR + its black frame.
3. AR.js tries to recognize the corresponding `.patt` file, displaying the selected Pokémon.
4. The two trainer QR URLs are independent, and the social-network QR on the left stays untouched.

## Files

- `assets/markers/{trainer}-qr-marker-19cm.svg`: printable vector, **190 mm × 190 mm** at 100% print scale.
- `assets/markers/{trainer}-qr-marker-19cm.png`: 300 DPI physical-size raster with QR and black border.
- `assets/markers/{trainer}-qr-15cm.png`: QR only (without black frame). For scan-only diagnostics; this image is **not** the AR marker.
- `assets/markers/{trainer}.patt`: experimental 16 × 16-pixel ARToolKit pattern templates (4 rotations and B/G/R channels).

`trainer` is `blaine` or `surge`. Do not alter the QR artwork after creating its `.patt`, or recognition may fail. Do not use a QR styling service that changes its pixels.

## Testing BEFORE permanent printing

- Display `...-qr-marker-19cm.svg` on a monitor, while opening the experience on the phone.
- Test QR scanning with the standard camera app: the URL should open the correct trainer.
- Start AR; aim the web camera back at the **same QR together with its complete black border**.
- Test marker tracking at increasingly realistic distances, angles and lighting.
- Test on Chrome Android and Safari iPhone; camera compatibility issues are still tracked in `docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md`.
- For HIRO comparison use `/pokemon/blaine/?marker=hiro` or `/pokemon/surge/?marker=hiro`.

**Caution:** AR.js compresses these marker interiors to a 16x16 template. A URL QR contains many high-frequency squares, which may make this marker hard to recognize. The `.patt` here is generated by a compatible 16x16 template algorithm but has not yet been verified with AR.js on a physical phone. If it fails, test the original official AR.js marker generator or switch recognition strategy, **without adding a third printed QR**.

**AR scale:** One AR marker unit is approximately the full physical marker width (19 cm). The previously calibrated HIRO values may be too small for a life-size Pokémon next to a person. V2.12 uses a separate per-card calibration stored as `jageekystuff-calibration-card-{pokemon}`; HIRO calibration remains intact. Calibrate only after successful marker detection and before final printing.

**Printing:** Print the SVG at **100% / actual size** (no fit-to-page) on a sufficiently large sheet. Verify with a ruler: the square must be exactly 19 cm wide and the inner QR square 15 cm. Avoid laminate glare and distortion. The 27 × 5.2 cm captions remain separate from this print.
